How the app works: the complete flow

From "site needs material" to "vendor is paid", who does each step and what happens next. Use − / + on a chart to zoom. (Needs internet once to draw the charts.)

1. Main flow: material to payment 2. Small site bills 3. Money and budget 4. Daily site work 5. Miko assistant 6. What triggers what 7. Who does what
Site team (Supervisor / Site Supervisor) Heads (Project Head / Head of Construction) Admins (Admin 1, Admin 2…) Accounts team Vendor App does it automatically 🔔 People get notified

1. Main flow: from material request to vendor payment

After the PO is approved, two tracks run side by side: goods (left) and money (right).

flowchart TD
  START(["Site needs material or a service"]):::site
  START --> SMALL{"Small buy, paid at the shop,
up to ₹5,000?"}
  SMALL -- "Yes" --> SB["Go to chart 2:
Site Bill"]:::site
  SMALL -- "No" --> MR["Write a Material Request
what · how much · which vendor · rough rate"]:::site
  MR --> N1["🔔 Project Head and
Head of Construction notified"]:::bell
  N1 --> A1{"Approve the request?
(one head is enough)"}:::head
  A1 -- "No" --> R1["Request rejected
raiser sees the reason"]:::bad
  A1 -- "Yes" --> KIND{"Regular vendor or
one-off supplier?"}

  KIND -- "One-off
(transport, small local buy)" --> NV1["Buy it, upload the bill"]:::site
  NV1 --> NV2(["Request closed"]):::auto

  KIND -- "Regular vendor" --> Q1["App creates the first quotation
from the request's vendor, items and rates"]:::auto
  Q1 --> Q2["Optional: add quotations
from other vendors to compare"]:::site
  Q2 --> Q3{"Compare and pick the best
(Finalize)"}:::head
  Q3 -- "Not this one" --> QR["Marked 'not selected'"]:::bad
  Q3 -- "Finalize" --> Q4["Other waiting quotations auto-closed
once every material is covered"]:::auto
  Q3 -- "Finalize" --> PO["App raises the Purchase Order (PO)
automatically"]:::auto
  PO --> N2["🔔 Heads notified"]:::bell

  A2 -- "No" --> R2["PO sent back:
edit and resend"]:::bad
  A3 -- "No" --> R2
  R2 --> N2
  A3 -- "Yes" --> OK["PO APPROVED

PO PDF ready to send to the vendor"]:::auto

  %% ── Goods track
  OK --> G1["Vendor sends the material"]:::vendor
  G1 --> G2["Truck arrives: make a Gate Pass

  G2 --> N4["🔔 Heads notified"]:::bell
  N4 --> G3{"Head / Admin approves

  G4 --> N5["🔔 Heads notified"]:::bell
  N5 --> G5["Inspect quality:

  G6 --> G7["Stock goes up

  M6 --> M7["Bank / cash balance goes down

told it is paid"]:::bell

whole story on one page in Combined Records"]):::auto

  • Budget head and payment type can be changed on a PO at any time without sending it back for approval.

5. Miko, the assistant (Telugu / English)

A shortcut to create records by chatting or sending a photo. It only prepares records; you confirm, and then the normal flow above takes over.

flowchart LR
  A(["Tap the robot icon"]):::site --> B["Pick what to make
or just type / speak / send a photo of a bill"]:::site
  B --> C["Miko reads the photo
and asks only what is missing
(tap-able answers)"]:::auto
  C --> D["Shows a card with
everything filled in"]:::auto
  D --> E{"You confirm?"}:::site
  E -- "Yes" --> F["Saved, exactly as if you made it yourself
(request, quotation, PO, receipt,
invoice, stock entry, site bill, vendor)"]:::auto
  E -- "No" --> G["Nothing saved"]:::auto
  F --> H["Normal approvals and
notifications follow (chart 1)"]:::bell
  F --> B
  NOTE["Miko can never approve,

6. What triggers what

Every "when this happens → the app does that" in one list.

When this happens……the app automatically does thisWho gets notified
Material Request is sentPuts it in the heads' approval list🔔 Project Head, Head of Construction
Material Request is approved (regular vendor)Creates the first quotation from the request🔔 Heads
Material Request is approved (one-off supplier)Opens it for uploading the bill; then it closes-
A quotation is finalizedRaises the Purchase Order; once all materials are covered, closes the other quotations as "not selected"🔔 Heads (PO to approve)
Head approves a POMoves it to the admins🔔 Admins
Admin approves a POReserves the amount on the budget head; PO is ready for gate pass, receipt and payment🔔 PO raiser + heads
Someone approves or rejects anythingRecords who and when; shows how long heads and admins took🔔 Raiser + heads (never the approver themselves)
Gate pass is madeWaits for OTP approval🔔 Heads
Goods receipt is recordedWaits for inspection🔔 Heads
Goods receipt is postedStock goes up, equipment becomes QR-tracked assets, PO shows part / fully delivered-
Invoice is enteredReads the photo to fill details; waits for verification🔔 Heads
Invoice is verifiedAdds the amount owed to the vendor's account; budget "billed" goes up-
Payment request is raisedWaits for admin approval🔔 Admins
Payment is madeBank / cash balance goes down, books and budget "paid" updated, confirmation ready to share🔔 PO raiser + request raiser (site bills: each bill owner)
Stock falls below its minimumShown as low stock on the dashboard-
Quotation waiting more than 24 hoursHourly check sends a reminder (once per quotation)🔔 Admins
Anything is created or changedSaved in the audit / activity log; searchable from the top search bar right away-
